Shiprocket Add-on for SnapBuy - Courier Shipping for eCommerce Orders

The Shiprocket add-on connects your SnapBuy store to Shiprocket's courier network, so every eCommerce order can be priced, shipped and tracked without leaving your admin panel.

Customers see live delivery charges and pincode availability before they buy. Your team books shipments with one click, prints labels and manifests in bulk, and lets tracking update orders automatically, from the first scan to the doorstep.

It is an add-on for SnapBuy 1.4.0 or later, built for stores shipping within India. Quick Commerce keeps running on your own delivery boys, while Shiprocket takes eCommerce orders nationwide.

Why Add Shiprocket to SnapBuy?

Turn eCommerce shipping into a few clicks: live rates at checkout, one-click shipments and tracking that keeps every order up to date.

Live Courier Rates at Checkout

Charge customers what delivery really costs. SnapBuy asks Shiprocket for live rates on every eCommerce checkout and adds your markup, rounding and minimum charge.

One-Click Shipments

Book items with Shiprocket straight from the order. Compare couriers by price, delivery time and rating, then create the shipment in one click.

Automatic Waybills & Pickups

The AWB is assigned the moment a shipment is created, and courier pickups can be requested automatically or with one click.

A Shipments Desk for Dispatch

Every parcel sorted by stage, with bulk labels, manifests, delivery receipts and pickups for the whole day's dispatch.

Tracking That Updates Itself

Shiprocket webhooks and a 30-minute sync move order items to Shipped, Out for Delivery and Delivered, while customers get a Track Shipment link.

Failed Deliveries & Returns Handled

Re-attempt or return failed deliveries, book return pickups and settle parcels the courier brings back, all from SnapBuy.

Install, Connect and Start Shipping

Install the add-on from the Plugins page, enter your Shiprocket API user, add a webhook token and choose whether waybills and pickups run automatically. Test everything in Sandbox mode before you switch to Live.

Delivery Charges from Real Courier Rates

Set any eCommerce zone to live courier rates and checkout prices delivery from Shiprocket's real rates for the parcel. Add a percentage or fixed markup, round up and set a minimum charge, while your free-delivery threshold still applies.

Pincode Check on Product Pages

Shoppers, even guests, check delivery to their pincode before adding to cart. The website shows the courier's estimated delivery date, and unserviceable pincodes are flagged before checkout.

Every Store Becomes a Pickup Address

Check whether couriers collect from a store's pincode and register the store with Shiprocket from the store form. Each store ships from its own address, so multi-store businesses work out of the box.

Ship with Shiprocket, Right from the Order

Pick the items, check the parcel's weight and volumetric weight, compare live courier rates with ETA, rating and COD support, and create the shipment without leaving the order.

One Shipments Desk for Every Parcel

A dedicated Shipments page groups parcels into To Dispatch, Awaiting Waybill, Ready to Ship, In Transit, Delivered and Needs Attention, with bulk printing, pickups and tracking refresh.

Tracking That Updates Orders by Itself

Every courier scan reaches SnapBuy through a webhook or the scheduled sync. Order items move forward on their own, customers get a Track Shipment link, and you choose when they're notified.

Failed Deliveries and Returns, Handled

When a delivery attempt fails, choose Try Delivery Again or Return to Origin. Accepted returns can be picked up with Shiprocket, and parcels that come back are settled your way.

Your Riders for Minutes, Shiprocket for Nationwide

Quick Commerce keeps using your own delivery boys. For eCommerce, each zone chooses Shiprocket, delivery boys or manual tracking, with prepaid and cash on delivery both supported.
