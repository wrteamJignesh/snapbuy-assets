SnapBuy: Hyperlocal Quick Commerce & eCommerce Platform with Multi-Store & Multi-Country

SnapBuy is a complete, source-code-included commerce platform for one brand that owns its stores and stock. It runs Quick Commerce (delivery in minutes from the nearest store) and eCommerce (item-wise fulfilment and shipping) from one codebase, across multiple stores and countries.

  • Flutter customer app for Android & iOS
  • Flutter delivery partner app for Android & iOS
  • Next.js web store
  • Laravel + Vue admin panel with REST API, store panels and a delivery boy web panel
